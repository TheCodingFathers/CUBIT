# Pressure Plate

## Overview

The Pressure Plate is a puzzle mechanism activated when CUBIT or another required object stands on it.

## Visual Design

- Futuristic platform-mounted mechanism
    
- Clean geometric design
    
- Navy and blue structural accents
    
- Cyan or orange activation indicators
    
- Compact design that fits naturally into the environment
    

## Core Behavior

- Detects objects standing on its surface
    
- Activates connected puzzle mechanisms
    
- Provides visual feedback when activated
    
- Works as part of the game's puzzle systems
