# ROTATE

## Overview

ROTATE is one of CUBIT's core gameplay mechanics. It allows the player to rotate designated puzzle elements to change their orientation.

## Gameplay Role

- Changes the orientation of designated puzzle elements
    
- Redirects energy beams through mirrors
    
- Helps solve environmental puzzles
    
- Combines with SHIFT in more advanced levels
    

## Controls

- R — ROTATE
    

## Design Principles

- Rotation should be visually clear.
    
- Objects should rotate predictably.
    
- The mechanic should remain consistent across levels.
    
- Challenges should progressively introduce more complex applications.
