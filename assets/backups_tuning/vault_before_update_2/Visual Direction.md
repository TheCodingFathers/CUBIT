# Visual Direction

## Overview

CUBIT: Core of the Cosmos uses a clean, futuristic, cubic visual style. The game's visual identity should remain consistent across its characters, environments, puzzle mechanisms, and UI.

## Art Style

- Stylized low-poly 3D
    
- Clean geometric forms
    
- Cubic and block-based shapes
    
- Minimal visual clutter
    
- Readable silhouettes
    
- Futuristic sci-fi atmosphere
    
- Simple but distinctive designs
    

## Color Palette

|Color|Hex|Purpose|
|---|---|---|
|Navy|#0a1230|Main structural color|
|Blue|#2563eb|Secondary surfaces and accents|
|Cyan|#2de2ff|Energy effects and highlights|
|Orange|#ff8a1f|Interactive elements and energy focal points|

## Design Principles

- Prioritize gameplay readability
    
- Keep mechanisms visually distinguishable
    
- Avoid unnecessary details
    
- Use colors consistently
    
- Maintain low polygon counts
    
- Prioritize performance on low-end hardware
