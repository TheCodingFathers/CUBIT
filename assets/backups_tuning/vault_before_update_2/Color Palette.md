# Color Palette

## Official CUBIT Colors

### Navy

- Hex: #0a1230
    
- Role: Primary structural color
    
- Used for: Main body surfaces, outlines, dark backgrounds
    

### Blue

- Hex: #2563eb
    
- Role: Secondary color
    
- Used for: Platform surfaces, mechanical components, accents
    

### Cyan

- Hex: #2de2ff
    
- Role: Energy and highlight color
    
- Used for: Energy beams, glowing elements, active mechanisms
    

### Orange

- Hex: #ff8a1f
    
- Role: Interactive and energy focal color
    
- Used for: Energy apertures, puzzle indicators, important focal points
    

## Color Rules

- Navy is the main foundation.
    
- Blue provides visual depth and contrast.
    
- Cyan communicates energy and activation.
    
- Orange highlights important interactive elements.
    
- Avoid introducing new major colors without a design decision.
