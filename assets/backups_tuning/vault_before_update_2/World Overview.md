# World Overview

## Overview

CUBIT takes place in the Cubiverse, a mysterious cubic dimension containing fragmented environments and puzzle-based challenges.

## World Structure

The game is planned around four worlds.

|World|Name|Palette|Background color|Levels|Status|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|World 01|The Awakening|Cyan and blue|`#0a1230` deep navy|11|In Development|
|World 02|The Fracture|Orange and red|`#2a0d08` dark red-brown|0|Placeholder|
|World 03|The Void|Purple and dark navy|`#120a2a` dark purple-navy|0|Placeholder|
|World 04|The Core|Gold, white and cyan|`#0b1a2e` navy with gold light|0|Placeholder|

Names and palettes come from the game design document and `src/worlds.js`. Worlds 02-04 are named and have lighting colors, but no levels or art yet. They show as "COMING SOON" in the level select.

## World 01 — The Awakening

World 01 introduces the player to CUBIT's core gameplay systems and puzzle mechanics.

It contains 11 levels:

1. First Steps
    
2. A Place to Stand
    
3. Turn the World
    
4. Connected
    
5. The First Fragment
    
6. Echoes
    
7. Flip Side
    
8. Hold and Move
    
9. Over Your Head
    
10. The Whole Machine
    
11. Reconnect
    

## Visual Direction

- Floating cubic platforms
    
- Futuristic sci-fi atmosphere
    
- Clean geometric environments
    
- Navy, blue, cyan, and orange palette
    
- Clear gameplay readability
