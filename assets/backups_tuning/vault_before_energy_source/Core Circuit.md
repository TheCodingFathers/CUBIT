# Core Circuit

## Overview

CORE CIRCUIT is a puzzle system involving energy beams and interconnected mechanisms.

## Gameplay Role

- Uses energy beams to activate puzzle mechanisms
    
- Connects Energy Sources, Mirrors, and Receivers
    
- Requires the player to redirect energy toward objectives
    
- Integrates with other puzzle mechanics

## How It Works

- The beam follows the floor grid cell by cell and never travels farther than 18 cells
    
- A Mirror turns the beam 90 degrees; the beam is cut while a mirror is turning
    
- A Receiver ends the beam at its core; when powered it opens the doors linked to it
    
- A door opens only while every plate and every Receiver linked to it is active
    
- Introduced in Level 11 (Reconnect)
    

## Design Principles

- Energy paths should be visually readable.
    
- Mechanisms should communicate their states clearly.
    
- Beam interactions should be consistent.
    
- Puzzle solutions should feel logical.
