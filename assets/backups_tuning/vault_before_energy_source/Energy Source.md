# Energy Source

## Overview

The Energy Source is a puzzle mechanism that emits an energy beam used to activate other mechanisms in CUBIT.

## Visual Design

- Compact futuristic emitter
    
- Solid mechanical body
    
- Distinctive raised central component
    
- Orange aperture as the focal point
    
- Cyan energy beam
    
- Navy and blue structural accents
    

## Design Direction

The Energy Source should look distinct from the Receiver.

The emitter should communicate that it generates and projects energy, while the Receiver captures and contains it.
